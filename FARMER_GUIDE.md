# 🌱 Farmer's Guide: Choosing Your 3-Year Crop Plan

Welcome! You do **not** need any computer skills to use this tool. If you can tap buttons on a phone or point with a mouse, you can finish your 3-year crop rotation plan in **3 minutes**.

> **What is this tool?** Think of it as a farming notebook that already knows your region's rainfall history, heat waves, and soil tiredness—and helps you pick which 3 crops to plant over the next 3 years so your land stays fertile and your harvest stays steady.

---

## 1. How It Works in 5 Simple Steps

1. **Pick Your Region & Crops (`1. Setup`):**
   - Tap your language (`العربية`, `FR`, or `EN`) at the top right.
   - Choose your region and tap **✨ Suggest for my region** so the sliders adjust automatically for your local water and soil conditions.
   - Select the 3 crops you usually plant in **Year 1**, **Year 2**, and **Year 3**.
   - Tick the crops you allow on your farm (at least **3 crops**, including **one grain/cereal** like wheat or barley and **one bean/legume** like chickpea, lentil, or faba bean).
2. **Check Local Rain & Heat (`2. Climate Changes`):**
   - See your area's yearly rain, hot days (`>35 °C`), frost days (`<0 °C`), heavy rain days, and longest dry spell.
   - Move the **What-If Sliders** (for example, `-20%` less rain and `+2 °C` warmer) to see how your crops hold up in a tough year.
3. **Read the Best 3-Year Plans (`3. Rotation Ranking`):**
   - See the top-ranked 3-year rotations scored out of **100**, with plain-language risk warnings for every crop.
   - Tap **🖨️ Print or save as PDF** to take a paper sheet to the field or co-op.
4. **Compare With Your Usual Plan (`4. Usual vs Rank 1`):**
   - See your current 3-year rotation side by side with Rank 1 on **Water Need**, **Soil Health**, **Harvest Stability**, and **Estimated Income per hectare**.
5. **Explore the Region Map (`5. Region Map`):**
   - Tap any region marker on the map and toggle the rainfall-trend overlay to see which zones are drying fastest.

---

## 2. Why You Can Trust This Tool

- **Built on real farming rules:** It never puts two crops from the same family back to back (which builds up pests and root disease), and it always includes a legume to restore natural nitrogen in the soil.
- **Tested against bad weather:** When the screen says *"rank 1 stays first in 87% of runs"*, it means we tested that rotation across dozens of drier and hotter scenarios—and it still came out on top.
- **Respects your current rotation:** We never hide your usual plan. We score it fairly right next to our #1 recommendation so you can judge the difference yourself.
- **Honest about its limits:** Satellite soil and rain maps measure whole neighborhoods (`250 m` squares), not every stone in your field—so your own eyes and experience always come first.
